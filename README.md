# AION 2 Field Guide — Codex corrections

This repository receives public correction reports for the independent, unofficial AION 2 Global Field Guide built with Codex. It contains reporting instructions only. The source implementation is maintained separately and remains private. This project is not affiliated with or endorsed by NCSOFT.

## Report a correction

Open Issues → New issue → Content correction. Include the guide URL or path, exact statement, suggested correction, Global region and difficulty, patch or announcement context, observation date and timezone, and an original evidence URL or permitted screenshot. An AI answer alone is not evidence. Say unknown when a detail is unavailable.

Reports and attachments here are public. Do not include passwords, account identifiers, personal contact details, payment information or private screenshots. GitHub may require sign-in. Opening the form does not submit it; you choose whether to publish the report.

The maintainer reviews evidence before changing a guide. Submission does not automatically publish a correction or guarantee a response time. Conflicting or outdated claims may be withdrawn while reviewed. Please keep discussion specific to the cited Global claim.

## Scope

Game outcomes and notices can change. Keep Global separate from KR/TW and Hard difficulty separate from Normal. No accounts, payments or gameplay services are offered here. Do not upload unlicensed game art or third-party material you do not have permission to share.
